1.3Portal par banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Form 49A select karo: Portal par "New PAN - Indian Citizen (Form 49A)" chuno. Correction ke liye "Changes or Correction in PAN" wala option.
  2. Category + token: Individual select karke application token generate karo, phir "Submit digitally through e-KYC & e-Sign (Paperless)" choose karo.
  3. Details bharo: Naam Aadhaar se EXACT same likho — bich me space, spelling, order kuch bhi change na ho. Yahi sabse bada rejection reason hai.
  4. AO code: Customer ke shehar ka AO code chuno (portal ke AO list me search karo). Galat AO code se application atak jati hai.
  5. Aadhaar e-KYC: Aadhaar number dalo — OTP customer ke linked mobile par jayega. Customer se hi turant phone/WhatsApp par OTP bolwao aur portal par dalo.
  6. Photo & signature upload: Photo 3.5x2.5 cm, 20-50 KB, white background; signature khaali page par black ink se, 20-50 KB. System se aayi photos quality check kar lo.
  7. DOB proof upload (2026 naya rule): Aadhaar ke alawa DOB ka alag proof ab MANDATORY hai — 10th marksheet, birth certificate, voter ID, driving licence, passport, marriage certificate ya magistrate affidavit. Aadhaar ab DOB proof ke liye accept NAHI hota (SarkariDNA video). System ye document WhatsApp par maang chuka hoga.
  8. Fee payment: Rs 107 portal par (ye customer ke payment me shaamil hai). Payment mode "Demand Draft" nahi — online/card/UPI chuno.
  9. Acknowledgement: 15-digit acknowledgement number save karke customer ko WhatsApp par bhejo. Ye tracking number hai.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Naam mismatch (Aadhaar vs form) = 60% rejections ka reason. Do baar match karo.
  • Minor applicant ke case me parent ka document aur "representative assessee" details lagti hain.
  • Correction request me purana PAN number bhi customer se le lo aur purana card ki photo bhi.
  • 15 din me PAN na aaye to acknowledgement number se NSDL helpline/timeline check karo.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Fee ka asli hisaab: physical card par govt fee Rs 107 hai; sirf e-PAN (PDF) Rs 66-72. Customer ko pehle poochho — e-PAN sasta aur 1-2 din me email par, physical card 4-6 din post se (SarkariDNA 2026 video).
  • Aadhaar ab DOB proof me kaafi NAHI (SarkariDNA PAN video): 2026 se Protean portal par DOB ke liye alag document lagta hai — 10th marksheet, birth certificate, voter ID, DL, passport, marriage certificate ya affidavit. Ye field system me add ho gayi hai (Sawal 8/8), document bhi maanga jayega.
  • Draft save karte raho: form bharne ke dauran "Save Draft" dabate raho — portal session expire hone par data loss nahi hota. e-KYC + e-Sign dono customer ke Aadhaar OTP se hote hain.
  • Minor PAN (SarkariDNA guide): bachche ke liye birth certificate + parent ka Aadhaar lagta hai; parent hi "representative assessee" banta hai. Bachche ki photo me aankhein khuli honi chahiye.
  • Naam ka exact match: Aadhaar me jaisa naam, wahi form me — spelling, space, order sab same. Isi ek galti se sabse zyada applications reject hote hain.

Income Tax Return (ITR) me customer apni saal ki income, TDS aur tax declare karta hai — jisme TDS jyada kata ho to refund bhi milta hai. Salaried employees ke liye ITR-1 (Sahaj) aur chhote business ke liye ITR-4 (presumptive) sabse common forms hain. Return file hone ke baad e-verification karna zaroori hai, warna return invalid maana jata hai.

2.3Portal par filing ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Documents cross-check: Form 16 ka salary + TDS bank statement se match karo. Koi mismatch ho to pehle customer se clear karo.
  2. Portal login: incometax.gov.in par customer ke PAN se login. Password customer ka hai — customer se khud type karwao, password kabhi apne paas na rakho.
  3. AIS/26AS dekho: Annual Information Statement me reported income aur TDS check karo — isme koi unknown entry ho to customer se poochho.
  4. Form chuno: ITR-1 (salary + 1 house property + interest), ITR-4 (business 44AD/44ADA). Category galat hui to notice aa sakta hai.
  5. Regime compare: Old vs New regime ka tax compare karo — portal auto-compute karta hai. Customer ko benefit wala option batado.
  6. Details fill: Income, deductions (80C, 80D agar old regime), TDS schedule, bank details. Aadhaar-bank link verified hona chahiye.
  7. Submit + e-Verify: Submit ke turant baad e-verification Aadhaar OTP se karo (OTP customer ke mobile par). 30 din ke andar e-verify zaroori, warna return invalid.
  8. ITR-V bhejo: Acknowledgement download karke customer ko WhatsApp par bhejo — refund status ke liye ye number kaam aayega.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • e-Verify bhulna sabse common galti hai — submit ke baad hi karwa lo, baad me na chhodo.
  • Bank account pre-validated na ho to refund atakta hai — pehle validation karwao.
  • Due date (non-audit) 31 July; late filing par 234F ke under Rs 1,000-5,000 fee lagti hai.
  • Interest income 10,000 se upar ho to ITR zaroori hai, salary kam bhi ho to.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • AIS/26AS reconciliation: Form 16 ka TDS aur bank interest ko AIS aur Form 26AS se match karo — mismatch par defective return notice (139(9)) aa jata hai.
  • Regime compare: portal old vs new regime auto-compute karta hai — jis me tax kam ho wahi chuno. New regime me 80C/80D deductions nahi chalti.
  • Refund ki 3 preconditions: PAN-Aadhaar linked, bank account pre-validated, aur return e-verified — ek bhi miss hua to refund atak jata hai.
  • AY 2026-27 ke naye rules (video course): Income Tax Act 2025 lagu — new regime me Section 87A rebate aur marginal relief ka fayda check karo; 5 income heads (Salary, House Property, PGBP, Capital Gains, Other Sources) ka sahi classification karo.
  • Form selection quick guide: ITR-1 salaried ke liye, ITR-2 capital gains/multiple property wale ke liye, ITR-4 presumptive business (44AD/44ADA) ke liye. Capital gains wale ITR-1 me na bharo — notice aayega.

3.3Portal par registration ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Part-A (REG-01): gst.gov.in ke Services, Registration, New Registration me jaao. Taxpayer type (Proprietorship etc.), PAN, mobile, email dalo — TRN generate hoga.
  2. TRN se login: TRN 15 din valid hota hai. TRN se login karke mobile + email dono par OTP verify karo (customer ke devices par aayenge).
  3. Part-B bharna: Business details, trade name, HSN/SAC code, principal address (state, district, jurisdiction officer auto-select hota hai).
  4. Address proof: Electricity bill (3 mahine purana nahi), rent agreement + owner ka bill, ya property tax receipt. Ye docs system ke alawa customer se lo.
  5. Promoter details: Proprietor ka photo, Aadhaar, PAN, personal address, bank account number + IFSC. Bank statement 3 mahine ka upload.
  6. Verification: Authorized signatory select karke EVC (Aadhaar OTP) se submit karo — ARN number turant milta hai.
  7. ARN track: Officer query aaye to 7 din me jawab dena hota hai, warna application reject. Approved hote hi GSTIN aur certificate email par aa jata hai.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Address proof sabse zyada query ka reason hai — naya electricity bill aur clear rent agreement rakho.
  • Trade name me special characters ya galat spelling se jurisdiction issue hota hai.
  • Query ka jawab 7 din me dena mandatory — deadline miss hua to application cancel.
  • Registration ke baad 6 mahine me pehla return bhi file karna zaroori hai, warna GSTIN suspend.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Aadhaar authentication karwao: proprietor ke Aadhaar OTP se authentication ho gayi to physical verification skip hoti hai aur approval 3-7 din me aa jata hai.
  • HSN/SAC sahi chuno: turnover ke hisaab se digits badhte hain (5 crore tak: 4-digit HSN) — galat code se baad me notice aata hai.
  • Certificate REG-06: approve hote hi GST certificate email par aata hai — customer ko PDF bhejo aur dukan par lagane ko kaho.
  • Aadhaar authentication opt-in karo (SarkariDNA GST video): Rule 14A ke under Aadhaar authentication chuno — isse physical verification skip hoti hai aur GSTIN kuch ghanton me issue ho jata hai, form bharne wale din hi.
  • Place proof ke 3 options: apni jagah hai to electricity bill (owner ka NOC rent me ho to agreement + bill), rented ho to rent agreement + owner ka bill. Ye docs mandatory hain — system ab WhatsApp par ye alag se maangta hai.
  • EVC ya DSC: proprietorship me EVC (Aadhaar OTP) se verify hota hai; company/LLP me DSC (digital signature) lagta hai. Submit ke baad registered email par e-KYC link bhi verify karna hota hai.

GST Return Filing

Registered business ko har mahine (ya QRMP scheme me quarterly) returns file karni hoti hain — GSTR-1 me sales invoices aur GSTR-3B me tax ka summary aur payment. Turnover 5 crore se kam wale QRMP chun sakte hain: sales quarterly, tax payment monthly. Late filing par daily late fee lagti hai aur customer ka ITC bhi ruk jata hai.

4.3Return filing ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Data lo: Customer se sales register aur purchase invoices Excel/PDF me lo. B2B (GSTIN wale) aur B2C (consumer) invoices alag karo.
  2. GSTR-1: Portal login karke Returns Dashboard me period chuno. B2B invoices ek-ek karke (ya offline tool se JSON upload), B2C ka summary rate-wise dalo.
  3. GSTR-3B: Auto-drafted liability check karo — outward tax, ITC claim, aur net payable. ITC tabhi claim karo jab supplier ne apni GSTR-1 file ki ho (2B me dikhta hai).
  4. Tax payment: Payable ho to electronic cash challan banao — customer ke net banking/UPI se pay karwao (ye customer ka direct payment hai, aapke payment me nahi).
  5. Late na karo: GSTR-1 ki 11 tareekh aur 3B ki 20 tareekh (QRMP me 22) hard deadline hai. Late fee Rs 50/day (nil return me Rs 20/day) per return lagti hai.
  6. Acknowledgement bhejo: Filed return ka ARN/reference number customer ko WhatsApp par bhejo aur next month ki date yaad dila do.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Supplier ne GSTR-1 file nahi ki to customer ka ITC claim nahi hoga — pehle supplier ko yaad dilao.
  • Nil return bhi file karna zaroori hai — file na karne par GSTIN status "pending" ho jata hai.
  • Invoice number duplicate ya gap na ho — sequential numbering check karo.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Interest ka hisaab: tax late paye to 18% annual interest lagta hai — 3B time par bharna sirf late fee se nahi, interest se bhi bachata hai.
  • QRMP option: turnover 5 crore se kam wale GSTR-1 quarterly (13 tarikh) + 3B monthly chun sakte hain — chhote dukaan walo ke liye aasan.
  • E-invoice: turnover 5 crore+ ho to e-invoice (IRN) mandatory hai — aise customers ko pehle se bata do.
  • GSTR-1 ke important tables (SarkariDNA filing video): Table 6 exports, Table 7 B2C supplies, Table 14/15 e-commerce (Swiggy/Zomato jaise ECO ke through bechne wale) — galat table me entry se mismatch notice aata hai.
  • Galti ho jaye to GSTR-1A: GSTR-1 file hone ke baad bhi GSTR-3B se pehle GSTR-1A me amendment karke B2C invoice values sudhar sakte ho — 3B hamesha sudhar ke baad file karo.
  • Rule 86B yaad rakho: jis month taxable supply 50 lakh cross kare to output tax ka kam se kam 1% cash me dena padta hai — ITC ke bharose sirf adjust nahi hota.
  • RCM ka hisaab alag rakho: import of services, GTA jaise reverse charge supplies 3B me alag table me aate hain — ye 2B se auto-populate hota hai ya manual calculate hota hai, ITC iska claim kar sakte ho.

5.3Certificate banane ka process (Operator SOP)
