Guide Kaise Use Karein

Ye guide CSC Smart Seva system ka operator manual hai. System customer se WhatsApp par hi pura kaam leta hai — service pata karna, fields bharna, documents lena aur payment lena. Uske baad government portal par form bharna, OTP/CAPTCHA dalna aur submit karna aapka (operator ka) manual kaam hai. Har service ke section me ye 7 cheezein milengi: service ka description, eligibility, system me configured fields, documents list, portal par step-by-step process, "Sahi Tarike" box (asli process ke facts — SarkariDNA video walkthroughs aur official portals se verify kiye hue), aur operator tips jo rejection se bachate hain.

Is edition (V3.0) me 9 step-by-step video walkthroughs ka knowledge seedha guide me merge kiya gaya hai — PAN card (Protean portal), Aay/Jati/Niwas Praman Patra (e-District), Ration Card (Smart PDS 2.0), GST registration aur GSTR-1/3B filing, ITR filing (AY 2026-27 ke naye rules) aur Passport apply. Har covered service ke "Sahi Tarike" box me video ka link bhi diya gaya hai — customer ke sawal ka jawab video ke timestamp ke saath check kar sakte ho. Videos dekhne ke liye Quick Reference ke baad wali Video Guide References table use karo.

Knowledge verification ka source: SarkariDNA YouTube channel (youtube.com/@SarkariDNA) ke sarkari services guide videos — is guide me 9 videos ka process seedha verify karke dala gaya hai (PAN, Aay, Jati, Niwas, Ration Card, GST Reg, GST Filing, ITR, Passport) — plus official portals (onlineservices.nsdl.com, incometax.gov.in, gst.gov.in, nfsa.gov.in, passportindia.gov.in, voters.eci.gov.in, beneficiary.nha.gov.in, register.eshram.gov.in, scholarships.gov.in). Isliye jo tareeka is guide me likha hai, wahi portal par asli me chalta hai.

System ka flow simple hai. Customer WhatsApp par message bhejta hai (jaise "pan card banana hai") — AI engine service pehchan leta hai aur fee bata deta hai. Customer CONFIRM karte hi ek-ek karke sawal aate hain, jisme galat jawab turant reject ho jata hai (jaise 12 digit Aadhaar, 10 digit mobile check). Documents photo/PDF me aate hain aur pricing ke baad UPI payment link jata hai. Payment webhook se verify hote hi application number banta hai (CSC-2026-XXXXXX) aur aapke operator WhatsApp par task aata hai. Aap portal par kaam complete karke DONE command se mark karte hain — customer ko confirmation chali jati hai.

1.15 Golden Rules — Ye Kabhi Na Todein

Rule 01
OTP aur CAPTCHA hamesha customer ke saath
Kisi bhi service ka OTP, CAPTCHA ya password automate nahi karte. Portal par OTP customer ke Aadhaar-linked mobile par hi aayega — wo khud dekh kar bolega. Customer se OTP puchna ya share karna mana hai; ye compliance aur trust dono ke liye critical hai.
Rule 02
Payment sirf official link se maano
Payment verify sirf system ke webhook se hota hai. Customer screenshot bheje to usse proof ke roop me kabhi mat lo — "payment ka intezar karein, verification automatic hai" bolo. Fake screenshot fraud ka sabse common tareeka hai.
Rule 03
Sensitive data masked rakho
Aadhaar number chat me bhi mask karke likho (XXXX XXXX 1234). Pura number, password ya OTP kisi bhi message, task note ya receipt me repeat na karo. Documents sirf system ke secure storage me rehte hain.
Rule 04
Duplicate application se bacho
Naya kaam shuru karne se pehle customer ka purana application STATUS se check karo. Ek hi service ke do applications se government fee do baar kat sakti hai aur refund mushkil hota hai. Application number hi single source of truth hai.
Rule 05
Fees aur rules go-live par verify karo
Is guide me fees system ki pricing table se aayi hain. Government portal ke fee aur rules time-to-time badalte hain — PORTAL_MODE LIVE karne se pehle aur mahine me ek baar official portal se verify karte raho.
Rule 06
Aadhaar-linked mobile pehla check hai
Ayushman, E-Shram, Voter ID, NSP — har e-KYC ka OTP customer ke Aadhaar-linked mobile par hi aata hai. Kaam shuru karne se pehle poochho: "ye number Aadhaar se linked hai?" Link nahi hai to pehle Aadhaar center bhejo — warna aadha kaam karke atak jayenge. Saath me customer ka DigiLocker account bhi bana do — documents hamesha safe aur valid rehte hain.
