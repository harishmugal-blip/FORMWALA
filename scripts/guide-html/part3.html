Birth Certificate
Civil Registry · CRSORGI / Municipal Portal
Rs 79
Customer Pays

Birth certificate school admission, Aadhaar, passport aur har legal kaam ki pehli zaroorat hai. Hospital me janm hue bachche ka record Civil Registration System (CRS) me already hota hai — hamara kaam us record se certificate nikalwana aur naam add karwana hota hai. Janm ke 21 din ke andar registration free hai; der ho jane par affidavit aur fine lagta hai.

8.3Certificate banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Hospital receipt check: Receipt par bachche ka provisional naam, janm ka date-time aur hospital ka medical officer in-charge ka naam hona chahiye.
  2. Record search: CRSORGI ya municipal portal par "birth search" me pita ka naam + janm date + hospital se record dhundo. Record mil gaya to direct certificate.
  3. Naam addition (agar nahi hai): Record me naam khali hota hai to "name inclusion" form bharo — dono parents ke Aadhaar, hospital receipt aur affidavit lagta hai. Iska time alag hota hai (1-2 mahine).
  4. Late registration: 21 din se 1 saal ke andar fine ke saath affidavit; 1 saal ke baad Executive Magistrate ka order lagta hai — ye customer ko pehle se bata do.
  5. Download + verify: Certificate me bachche ka naam, parents ke naam, janm date aur registration number verify karke bhejo.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Naam ki spelling final karke hi form bharo — baad me correction lamba process hai.
  • Ghar me hua janm ho to Asha worker/ANM ka report gram panchayat se lagwana hota hai.
  • School admission me aksar name-inclusion bhi saath chahiye hota hai — pehle hi poochho.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Naye niyam ka fayda (SarkariDNA guide): ab digital birth certificate hi school admission, DL, voter card, sarkari naukri aur passport ka single document maana jata hai — isliye ye sabse pehle banwana chahiye.
  • Digital download: certificate e-district/CRS portal se PDF me milta hai aur DigiLocker me bhi sync hota hai — print karke bhi le lo.
  • Ghar ka janm: ASHA worker/ANM ya pradhan ka report lagwao — hospital receipt nahi hoga to yahi proof banega.

Death Certificate
Civil Registry · CRSORGI / Municipal Portal

Customer Pays

Death certificate pension transfer, bank account settle karne, insurance claim aur property ke naam badalne ke liye zaroori legal document hai. Isme sensitivity ka dhyan rakhte hue jaldi aur sahi kaam karna hota hai — 21 din ke andar registration free hota hai. Hospital death summary aur cremation/burial certificate do main proof hain.

9.3Certificate banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Proof jama karo: Hospital death summary (hospital me hua ho) ya cremation ground ka receipt, swargiya ka Aadhaar aur aavedak ka Aadhaar.
  2. Record search: CRS portal par mrityu record search karo — pita/pati ka naam + date se. Medical officer ne record kiya hoga to seedha certificate.
  3. Application bharo: Swargiya ka pura naam, umar, mrityu ki date-sthaan, aavedak ka relation aur address. Ghar par mrityu hui ho to Asha/pradhan ka report.
  4. Submit + follow-up: Registrar verification ke baad certificate issue hota hai. Pension/bank ke liye 5-6 copies print karwao.
  5. Legal heir ke aage ke kaam: Customer ko batao ki pension transfer/insurance me death certificate + legal heir certificate dono lagenge (alag service hai).
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Naam Aadhaar ke EXACT matching se lo — bank/pension me mismatch hoti hai to kaam rukta hai.
  • 21 din ke andar register karwao — der se fine aur affidavit lagega.
  • Aavedak ka relation form me sahi likho (pita/pati/beta) — legal heir process isi se chalta hai.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Hospital death ka MCCD form: doctor se cause-of-death (MCCD) form lena zaroori hai — isi par record banta hai, cremation receipt sirf support karta hai.
  • 21 din ki deadline: andar register free; 1 saal tak fine ke saath; uske baad Magistrate order — ye time-sensitive kaam hai, turant karo.
  • Copies ka math: pension, insurance, bank, property — har jagah 1-2 certified copies chahiye; ek baar me 5-6 print nikalwao.

Voter ID (New / Correction)
Election · NVSP / Voters' Service Portal
Rs 59
Customer Pays

Voter ID (EPIC) vote dalne ka official card hai aur age+address ka strong proof bhi. Naye voter ke liye Form 6, correction ya address shift ke liye Form 8 lagta hai. Age 18+ honi chahiye qualifying date (1 January) ke hisaab se. Application ke baad BLO ka verification (ghar visit) hota hai, phir EPIC card 30-45 din me aata hai.

10.3Application ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Form chuno: Form 6 (naya voter), Form 8 (correction/shifting), Form 7 (objection). Correction me purana EPIC number zaroor lo.
  2. Portal login: voters.eci.gov.in par mobile number se registration + OTP. Aadhaar e-KYC se details auto-fill ho jati hain.
  3. Details bharo: Naam (English + local language), DOB, sambandhi ka naam relation ke saath, aur adhoolg/polling booth ke hisaab se address.
  4. Photo + docs: Live photo ya upload; age proof (birth certificate/10th marksheet/Aadhaar) aur address proof upload karo.
  5. Submit + reference: Application reference number customer ko bhejo. BLO ka naam portal par dikh jayega.
  6. BLO verification: BLO ghar aa kar verify karta hai — customer ko pehle se bata do taaki ghar par mile. Status portal par har hafte check karo.
  7. EPIC issue: Card post se aata hai; e-EPIC PDF turant download hota hai (Aadhaar-linked mobile se).
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Address galat constituency me dalne se application transfer/reject hoti hai — booth poochho.
  • Form 6 me umar DOB se kam aayi to age proof strong chahiye — 10th marksheet best.
  • Aadhaar-Voter linking bhi kara do (Form 6B) — election commission ki requirement hai.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Age-proof ki official list (ECI Form 6): birth certificate, Aadhaar, PAN, passport, ya 10th marksheet — inme se koi ek. Sabse strong: birth certificate ya marksheet.
  • 17 saal wale bhi apply karein: jo jald 18 ke honge wo Form 6 advance me bhar sakte hain — naam list me jaldi jud jata hai.
  • SIR/draft roll check (SarkariDNA guide): election se pehle voter list ka revision hota hai — naam list me hai ya nahi, voters.eci.gov.in par pehle check karo; asli voting entry list me naam hi hai.

Ayushman Card
Health · PM-JAY / NHA Beneficiary Portal
Rs 30
Customer Pays

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY) card se parivar ko har saal 5 lakh tak ka free hospitalization milta hai — empanelled government/private hospitals me. Card banane se pehle eligibility check hoti hai (SECC 2018 / state list me parivar ka naam hona chahiye), phir Aadhaar e-KYC aur ration card se family members add hote hain.

Kaun banwa sakta haiEligible list (SECC/state) me shamil parivar; Aadhaar-linked mobile zaroori
Processing timeSame day - 3 din (e-KYC smooth ho to turant)

11.3Card banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Eligibility check: Portal/app par mobile ya ration card se parivar search karo. Naam list me nahi to state ki Arogya Mitra desk se complaint/survey line batana hoga.
  2. Parivar verify: Ration card se family members ka naam match karo — jo members add nahi hain unki details portal par add hoti hain.
  3. Aadhaar e-KYC: Har adult member ka Aadhaar OTP verification — OTP customer ke mobile par aata hai, customer se hi le lo. Bachchon ke liye parent ka Aadhaar.
  4. Details confirm: Naam ki spelling, gender, DOB aur relation — ration card se exactly match karo, correction baad me mushkil hoti hai.
  5. Card download: Ayushman card PDF turant generate hota hai — print karke customer ko do aur hospital admission me ye card hi dikhana hota hai.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Ration card me naam alag ho to pehle ration card correction karwao — Ayushman usi se verify hota hai.
  • Aadhaar mobile link nahi hai to pehle bank/Aadhaar center me linking karwao.
  • Customer ko empanelled hospital ki list bhi WhatsApp kar do — card ke fayde ki puri jaankari milegi.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Vay Vandana 70+ (SarkariDNA guide): 70 saal se bade HAR citizen ka card banta hai — income proof ya ration card nahi chahiye, sirf Aadhaar se age verify hoti hai. Ye alag Rs 5 lakh cover hai, PM-JAY list walo ke saath.
  • Pehle eligibility check: portal par parivar ka naam na mile to card nahi banega — Arogya Mitra desk / state survey line batana hoga. Ration card correction pehle karwao.
  • OTP Aadhaar-linked mobile par: e-KYC fail ho to sabse pehle mobile-Aadhaar link check karo (Rule 06).
