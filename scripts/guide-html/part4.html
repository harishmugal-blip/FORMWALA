Labour · Ministry of Labour (UAN)

Customer Pays

E-Shram card unorganized sector workers (kheti, majdoori, rickshaw, dukaan, ghar ka kaam aadi) ka national database registration hai — 12-digit UAN number milta hai. 12.3Card banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Mobile-Aadhaar link check: Pehla step yahi hai — link nahi hai to e-KYC fail hoga. Customer ko pehle Aadhaar center bhejo ya biometric se karwa lo.
  2. Self-registration: Portal par mobile + Aadhaar e-KYC (OTP customer ke saath) karo. Registered mobile par OTP aayega.
  3. Personal details: Naam, DOB, gender, social category, education, marital status — Aadhaar se auto-populate hote hain, cross-check karo.
  4. Occupation + skills: Occupation dropdown se exact kaam chuno (55 options hain), skill aur kaam ki nature (self-employed/regular/casual) batao.
  5. Bank + address: Account number, IFSC, pura address aur nominee details. Photo upload (ya Aadhaar se aati hai).
  6. UAN download: Card turant PDF me aa jata hai — print karke customer ko do aur UAN number WhatsApp par bhejo.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • EPFO/ESIC member workers eligible nahi hote — pehle poochho kahan kaam karta hai.
  • Bank account e-Shram me zaroori hai (PMSBY claim isi se hoga) — passbook ka pehla page clear photo.
  • Card par accident insurance ke baare me customer ko zaroor batayein — ye sabse bada fayda hai.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Self-declaration portal hai: 400+ occupations me se dhanda chunna hai — koi proof nahi mangte, par galat jaankari par future scheme benefits ruk sakte hain.
  • Update bhi karwao: address, occupation ya mobile badla ho to e-Shram portal par update karwao — purane data par PMSBY claim atak jata hai.
  • Registration FREE hai: portal par koi govt fee nahi — customer ko bata do ki Rs 30 sirf service charge hai (bade CSC centers bhi 30-50 hi lete hain).

13.3Form bharne ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Notification padho: Vacancy ka official notification PDF khulo — post, vacancies, age limit, education, fees aur important dates note karo. Customer se eligibility match karo.
  2. Registration: Portal par naya registration (agar pehle nahi bana) — mobile + email se OTP. Registration ID/Roll number customer ko WhatsApp karo.
  3. Form details: Personal, address, education (marks exact certificate se), category, preference posts. Jo bhar chuke purane forms hain unse data match karo.
  4. Photo + signature spec: Har exam ki apni size/ratio hoti hai (photo 20-50KB, signature 10-20KB aam hai) — spec ke bahar upload reject ho jata hai.
  5. Exam fee: Customer ke debit/UPI se portal par pay karwao, ya sath beth ke customer khud kare. Fee receipt save karo.
  6. Final print: Application ka final print/PDF customer ko bhejo + exam date aur city slip ki jankari do. Correction window ki date bhi note karo.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Photo/signature ki size-spec galat = application reject — pehle spec padho, phir resize karo.
  • Category certificate current financial year ka maango (OBC/EWS me dated hota hai).
  • Naam 10th certificate se EXACT lo — baaki certificates me bhi wahi order follow hota hai.
  • Last date pe ek din pehle submit karwao — last-day rush me payment portal atak jata hai.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • OBC/EWS certificate dated hote hain: OBC non-creamy layer 1 saal ke andar ka, EWS current FY ka — purana certificate form me nahi chalega.
  • Photo spec aam hai: white background, passport size, 20-50 KB; kai exams me naam + date photo par likhi honi chahiye — notification me likha hota hai.
  • Preview + final submit: fee payment ke baad application PREVIEW dekh kar hi FINAL SUBMIT karo — partially submitted form reject maana jata hai.

14.3Application ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. OTR banao: scholarships.gov.in par One Time Registration — Aadhaar face-auth/OTP customer ke mobile se. OTR number lifetime ke liye same rehta hai.
  2. Scheme chuno: Class, category, income aur state ke hisaab se eligible scheme list dekho (pre-matric, post-matric, minority, merit). Har scheme ki deadline alag hoti hai.
  3. Academic details: Institute ka NSP code, course, current year, pichle exam ke marks (minimum percentage scheme me hota hai).
  4. Bank + documents: Account number + IFSC (student ke naam), Aadhaar-bank link check karo. Income certificate (fresh), caste certificate, marksheet, hostel/day-scholar declaration upload karo.
  5. Submit + institute verify: Application institute (Nodal officer) ke paas verification ke liye jayegi — principal/clerk ko jaldi se sign karwao, yahi sabse dheema step hai.
  6. State verification: Uske baad district/state level verification hoti hai. Status har hafte check karo — defect aa jaye to 15 din me theek karwana hota hai.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Deadline miss = pura saal gaya — October-December wali window board par likh lo.
  • Aadhaar-bank link na ho to DBT fail hota hai — pehle hi check karwao.
  • Income certificate fresh (current FY) chahiye — purani file nahi chalegi.
  • Institute NSP me registered hi hona chahiye — pehle principal office se confirm karo.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Admission proof + fee receipt bhi: income/caste certificate ke saath admission proof aur fee receipt bhi upload hote hain — school/college se le lo.
  • Fresh vs Renewal: pichle saal NSP lene wale ka RENEWAL (promotion certificate ke saath), naye students ka FRESH — dono ka portal path alag hai.
  • Account student ka khud ka: DBT student ke apne Aadhaar-linked account me hi hoti hai — parents ke account me paisa nahi aata.

15.3Portal par banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Citizen Login: Smart PDS portal par "Citizen Login" chuno — mukhiya ka Aadhaar number dalo, OTP unke linked mobile par aayega. Ye OTP customer ka hai.
  2. RC Application: "RC Application" me jaakar "Apply New Ration Card" select karo.
  3. Mukhiya details: Naam, photo (max 5MB), occupation, qualification aur income bharo — naam Aadhaar se exact match hona chahiye.
  4. Members add karo: Har family member ka Aadhaar number, relation aur occupation ek-ek karke add karo. Sabhi members ka Aadhaar WhatsApp par already mil chuka hoga.
  5. Bank + FPS dealer: Bank account details dalo aur Fair Price Shop (jahan se ration milega) ka dealer chuno — galat FPS chune to ration alag dukaan par jayega.
  6. Review + submit: Pura form ek baar review karke submit karo — acknowledgment number save karke customer ko bhejo. Yahi tracking number hai.
  7. Physical submission (agar lage): Kuch regions me application print karke local food inspector ko jama karni hoti hai — customer ko pehle bata do.
  8. Status track: Acknowledgment number se portal par status check karte raho; card banne ke baad customer ko bata do.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Pehle check karo ki parivar ke kisi member ka purana RC to nahi — duplicate application reject hoti hai.
  • Member add karne ka kaam "Add Member" flow se hota hai, naye card se alag — kaunsa kaam hai pehle confirm karo.
  • Income/caste certificate optional hain, par lagane se BPL/ANTYODAYA category milne ke chances badhte hain.
  • Aadhaar me ghar ka address purana ho to pehle address update karwao — RC usi par banega.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Smart PDS 2.0 + Aadhaar OTP (SarkariDNA Ration video): login ke liye Aadhaar + OTP hi process hai — koi password nahi. Isliye mukhiya ka Aadhaar-linked mobile sabse pehle confirm karo.
  • Photo max 5MB: mukhiya ki passport-size photo 5MB se chhoti honi chahiye; income/caste certificate aur address proof PDF me upload hote hain.
  • FPS dealer selection zaroori: form me hi ration dukaan (FPS) choose karni hoti hai — ye step skip hua to card bana par ration nahi milega.
  • Physical submission bhi ho sakti hai: video ke hisaab se kuch regions me print karke food inspector ko dena padta hai — acknowledgment number ke saath rakho.
  • Pura video: youtu.be/ZdpjzvyLfuQ — New Ration Card Apply 2026 (SarkariDNA)

Passport
Identity · Passport Seva (PSK)
Rs 2618
Customer Pays

Passport visa travel ke liye sabse strong identity + citizenship proof hai. Application Passport Seva portal par hoti hai — 9-step form, fee payment, phir PSK (Passport Seva Kendra) appointment jahan original documents verify hote hain aur photo/signature live capture hota hai. Uske baad police verification hoti hai. Form me hi ECR/Non-ECR aur 36/60 pages decide hota hai.

Processing timeAppointment ke baad 7-15 din (police verification sahit); tatkal 1-3 din
Fee breakdownGovt 2500 + Service 100 + GST 18 = Rs 2618 (36 pages, 10 saal, normal; tatkal extra)

Portal passportindia.gov.in AppointmentPSK slot — operator book karega PhotoPSK par live capture hota hai

16.3Portal par banane ka process (Operator SOP)

  1. Registration: passportindia.gov.in par "New User Registration" — customer ki email ID login banti hai, isliye email access confirm karo. Password customer ka hoga.
  2. Apply type chuno: Fresh/Re-issue me se, 36 ya 60 pages, Normal ya Tatkal. Pehli baar = Fresh, 36 pages kaafi hai.
  3. 9-step form: Personal details, family details, address (current + permanent), emergency contact, previous passport (agar hai) — ek-ek step bharo.
  4. ECR/Non-ECR: 10th pass hai to Non-ECR eligible — BELOW_10TH wale ECR me aate hain jinka process thoda alag hota hai.
  5. Fee payment: Rs 2500 online pay karo (video: fee pehle Rs 1500 thi, ab Rs 2500 ho gayi). Tatkal me extra Rs 2000 lagta hai.
  6. PSK appointment: Slot book karo — jaldi book hota hai, appointment slip print/WhatsApp par rakho. Appointment par original documents + ek photocopy set le jana zaroori.
  7. PSK visit: Photo aur signature wahan live capture hote hain — WhatsApp wali photo yahan nahi lagegi. Customer ko original documents wahi dena hota hai.
  8. Police verification: Ghar par police aayegi — address proof ready rakho. Uske baad passport post se aata hai; tracking number se status dekho.
Operator Tips — Rejection se bachav
  • Email login hi sab kuch hai — customer ka email access na ho to pura process atak jayega, pehle confirm karo.
  • Appointment ke din late pahunche to slot cancel ho jata hai — customer ko samay pehle pahunchne ko kaho.
  • Naam marksheet/Aadhaar me EXACT same ho — shaadi ke baad surname badla ho to marriage certificate lagta hai.
  • Police verification me ghar par na mile to verification "not found" ho jati hai — customer ko date pehle bata do.
Sahi Tarike — Asli Process Facts
  • Fee badh gayi — Rs 2500 (SarkariDNA Passport video): pehle Rs 1500 thi, ab normal 36-pages/10-saal passport ki fee Rs 2500 hai. System pricing isi hisaab se set hai.
  • ECR vs Non-ECR: 10th pass = Non-ECR (bina ECR check ke kisi bhi desh me travel); 10th fail = ECR category — kuch deshon me additional approval lagta hai.
  • Photo PSK par hi: online application me photo upload nahi hota — Passport Seva Kendra par live photo + signature capture hota hai. Isliye system WhatsApp par photo nahi maangta.
  • DOB proof alag se: Aadhaar ke saath DOB ka proof (birth certificate, 10th marksheet, voter ID) bhi laga do — birth certificate sabse strong hai.
  • Pura video: youtu.be/9FaahA_lGnY — Passport Apply Online 2026 (SarkariDNA)
